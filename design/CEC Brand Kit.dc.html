Debrief
Decor rules — background stickers live at 6–12% effective opacity, always blurred, never behind body text at less than 70% paper cover. One decor moment per screen. Forms, Admin and the Notes editor body get none. Functional stickers (flags, beads, sun, confetti) stay at full strength — they carry meaning.
